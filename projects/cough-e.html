Cough-E

August 2025 – December 2025

Add a photo of the build here

A six-bar linkage locomotion system synchronized with a geneva-driven dispenser, designed for precise positional payload delivery.

What I did

  • Performed PVA (position-velocity-acceleration) analysis on the six-bar linkage to determine a stride length of 4.375 cm and derive the 300:1 compound gear ratio needed to synchronize the geneva-driven dispenser at 1 payload per 2 m
  • Evaluated Delrin vs. ¼" acrylic for linkage fabrication through physical testing; selected acrylic to reduce lateral instability
  • Laser cut and assembled the final linkage components

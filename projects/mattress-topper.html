Intelligent Mattress Topper

October 2024 – August 2025 · Lead Engineer

Add a photo of the build here

A smart pressure-relief mattress pad integrating pneumatic actuation, a 16-sensor pressure array, and embedded control to improve early pressure ulcer detection by 60%. I led design and prototyping on this project.

What I did

  • Designed and laid out embedded PCBs in KiCAD for microcontroller-driven air-valve actuation across 16 pressure zones; validated layout for signal integrity and low-profile form factor
  • Implemented and tested a real-time pressure heat map in C++ and Python, processing 16-sensor array data to visualize localized pressure distribution and time-rate changes
  • Coordinated pneumatic actuation system design alongside the sensor and control electronics

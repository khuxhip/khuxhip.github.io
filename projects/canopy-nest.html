Canopy Nest

August 2024 – December 2024

Add a photo of the build here

An outdoor product redesign focused on part-count reduction and manufacturability, balancing durability against cost.

What I did

  • Produced a full CAD assembly and GD&T engineering drawings
  • Performed DFA analysis identifying a part count reduction from 10 to a theoretical minimum of 2, through geometry consolidation and process substitution
  • Selected materials and manufacturing methods — porcelain slip casting, steel roll forming — through DFM analysis balancing outdoor durability, corrosion resistance, and cost; the resulting design reduced projected waste by 30%

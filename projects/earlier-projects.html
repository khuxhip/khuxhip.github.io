Hydraulic Claw Machine

2023 - 2024 · Women in Mechanical Science and Engineering

Add a photo of the build here

The Hydraulic Claw Machine which is a Engineering Open House exhibit designed to demonstrate fluid-powered mechanical actuation through an arcade-style experience. Users operate connected syringes that control the claw's position and gripping motion, allowing them to retrieve prizes.

Project Highlights

  • Integrated syringe-and-tubing hydraulic circuits to convert user input into controlled linear motion for the claw mechanism.
  • Combined a wooden enclosure, mechanical linkages, hydraulic components, and 3D-printed parts into a full-scale arcade-style machine.
  • Integrated horizontal movement, vertical movement, and gripping into a single manually controlled mechanical system.

The final prototype allows users to position and operate the claw machine with separate hydraulic controls. It was presented as an interactive exhibit at Engineering Open House under the Women In Mechanical Engineering & Sciences Organization, where in visitors were able to obtain prizes from the machine. While I did not lead the project, I assisted designing and building the project!

Medsecure

2022 - 2023 · Minerva, PLTW

Add a photo of the build here

MedSecure is a medication-management prototype developed to help users follow prescription schedules and reduce the risk of missed or incorrect doses. Created by a team, the system combines medication storage, automated delivery, scheduled reminders, basic health monitoring, and an accessible interface.

Project Highlights

  • Designed a motor-driven conveyor mechanism to transport medication from dedicated storage compartments to a centralized dispensing location.
  • Used microcontroller logic to coordinate the dispenser, alarm system, display, and sensor inputs.
  • Implemented a visual display alert system to communicate scheduled dosage information and notify the user when medication was due.
  • Integrated a pulse sensor to collect heart-rate measurements as an additional patient-monitoring function.

The final prototype combines multiple medication-management subsystems into one physical design. Although the project remained an early-stage prototype, it demonstrated how mechanical and electronic components could be integrated into a single healthcare-focused product concept.

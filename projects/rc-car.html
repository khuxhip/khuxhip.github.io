RC Car

January 2026 – May 2026

stupid car pics go here

A multi-speed RC car built around a cam-actuated sliding mesh spur gear transmission, with high-speed (1:3), high-torque (9:1), and reverse gearing, controlled via an IR remote.

Introduction

  • Performed FEA on the TPU impact roof, validating structural integrity with an Impact Factor of 63.25 and a safety factor of 17.15 under a worst-case 3 kg drop from 0.25 m
  • Manufactured and assembled drivetrain components via 3D printing and hand tooling: gears, motor mounts, servo mounts, and the brake system
  • Designed the cam-actuated sliding mesh mechanism to allow smooth gear changes between high-speed and high-torque modes
  • This project was in completion for ME 371: Mechanical Design II, in which is a design and analysis course for loadbearing and power transmission. The project for this course is to design a car for a heptathlon-style competition, suiting the needs for the particular consumer assigned and performing in such categories including but not limited to speed, strength, impact resistance, and gearbox efficiency. The car design holds a cam-actuated sliding mesh spur gear transmission which is controlled via an IR remote, holding a high speed (1:3), high torque (9:1), and reverse mode.

Aim

  • For our consumer, Jordan, he wanted the car to have a compact and robust design that was cost-effective, with the defined set of performance goals: Reaching speeds over 1.25 m/s, carrying a 1 kg load up a ramp, staying under $25 in budget, and achieving at least 40% gearbox efficiency, all while maintaing a polished aesthetic.

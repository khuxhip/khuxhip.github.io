RC Car

January 2026 – May 2026

Add a photo of the build here

A multi-speed RC car built around a cam-actuated sliding mesh spur gear transmission, with high-speed (1:3), high-torque (9:1), and reverse gearing, controlled via an IR remote.

What I did

  • Performed FEA on the TPU impact roof, validating structural integrity with an Impact Factor of 63.25 and a safety factor of 17.15 under a worst-case 3 kg drop from 0.25 m
  • Manufactured and assembled drivetrain components via 3D printing and hand tooling: gears, motor mounts, servo mounts, and the brake system
  • Designed the cam-actuated sliding mesh mechanism to allow smooth gear changes between high-speed and high-torque modes
